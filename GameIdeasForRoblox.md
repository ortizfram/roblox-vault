# Game Ideas for Roblox — Living Research Playbook

**Status:** Active / Version 1.0  
**Created:** 2026-11-21  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-21  
**Main reference:** [Tizzy RBLX — I paid a top dev $300 to teach me how he finds Roblox game ideas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8q6kdFVL_fY)  
**Purpose:** Find **proven, easy-to-understand game fantasies that succeeded outside Roblox**, check whether Roblox has already served that demand, and prototype the best *distinctive* idea fast.

**AI index:** [README.md](README.md) → [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). **Antes de anuncios:** revisar [CampaingsAndPreGameCreation.md](CampaingsAndPreGameCreation.md) §1–4 para keyword/title/thumbnail/CTR y gates de retención; no duplicar información de campañas aquí.

> **North star:** Don't invent demand. Find a fantasy or mechanic people already love, verify its audience, identify the underserved Roblox version, and make it simple, social, funny, and instantly readable.
>
> **Important:** The criteria and inspiration sources below are from our working notes on the Tizzy video; the scoring model, workflow, and adaptation ideas are our research framework. The linked video itself was not transcribed for this document.

## 1. Mandatory idea filters

### A. Proof it worked outside Roblox

**Preferred hard filter:**

- [ ] Original game/fantasy **already exists outside Roblox**, with an identifiable original and source link.
- [ ] **3,000,000+ lifetime plays/visits/downloads** on a credible platform (be explicit about which metric). Don't add unrelated websites' counts together without evidence.
- [ ] Either **rating >=4.8/5** (equivalent to >=96/100 or >=9.6/10 when scales are comparable), **or** **rating >=9/10 with 200,000+ votes/ratings**.
- [ ] Capture the **rating count**, not just the rating: 4.8/5 from 10 people is far weaker than 4.8/5 from 3 million.
- [ ] Prefer **recent demand signals**: YouTube/Shorts/TikTok videos, creators still playing it, active community, mobile downloads, or search interest.
- [ ] Record the source, measurement date, and whether the number means *plays, downloads, visits, unique users, votes,* or *reviews*.

**Screenshot benchmark from our notes:** `4.8 / 5 (3,484,016 votes)`. This is an example of very strong rating-volume evidence; **the game shown in that cropped image is not identified**.

**Exception / Watchlist (not greenlit):** a game with 3M+ plays and 9/10+ rating but fewer than 200k votes, or a TikTok challenge with massive views but no game-rating data, can go into **Research Leads**. It **does not pass** the strict proof filter until supported by other evidence and explicitly approved as an exception.

### B. Roblox opportunity — verify, don't assume

- [ ] Search the **exact name**, **main action**, **fantasy**, **synonyms**, and **adjacent genres** on [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/discover).
- [ ] For all important matches, record: Roblox game link, genre, visits, current concurrent users (CCU), likes/approval, last update, thumbnail, core loop, and observation date. Do not invent missing numbers.
- [ ] Identify whether Roblox has: **(1) no meaningful version, (2) weak/old versions, (3) strong adjacent versions, or (4) a dominant direct competitor**.
- [ ] A zero search result **does not prove absence**. Search neighboring words and actual mechanics; visually play the likely matches.
- [ ] If the fantasy exists, specify **what is underserved** and what makes our implementation genuinely different. A near-identical clone is a no-go.
- [ ] Classify Roblox's existing version: `simulator`, `tycoon`, `obby`, `party`, `survival`, `PvP`, `co-op`, `horror`, `RNG/collection`, `roleplay`, `incremental`, `arena`, or `other`.

### C. Roblox-native gameplay

- [ ] **One-sentence fantasy** kids can repeat (“I launch my friend into space with a giant slingshot”).
- [ ] **One obvious action** in the first 5–10 seconds, ideally mobile-friendly.
- [ ] **Core loop** is satisfying even with grey blocks: `do action -> instant reaction/reward -> improve/unlock -> do it again`.
- [ ] **Social**: friends can help, compete, race, sabotage, show off, or laugh at each other.
- [ ] **Clip potential**: at least 3 believable funny, chaotic, or surprising moments in a 10-minute session.
- [ ] **Strong visual payoff**: big objects, reactions, numbers, explosions/confetti, transformations, ragdolls, knockbacks, disasters, or surprising chain reactions.
- [ ] **Simple version is fun**. Depth comes later; no long tutorial, complicated crafting tree, or realistic simulation as the first interaction.
- [ ] Target **2–3 days for an ugly playable core-loop prototype**, not necessarily a finished public release.
- [ ] Produce a **clear title and thumbnail concept** before committing. These, together with the core loop, form the first test of the idea. “Title + thumbnail are 50%” is a creative prioritization rule, **not a measured statistic**.

### D. Red flags

- No believable proof outside Roblox; high score with tiny sample; only historic demand and no modern audience.
- Roblox already has a dominant, identical execution and we have no twist.
- Fun requires reading, precision mouse controls, a long tutorial, complicated systems, or high-end hardware.
- No reason to return after five minutes; only one joke with no replayability.
- Prototype requires months of unique animations, maps, 3D assets, networking, or advanced AI.
- Based on unsafe real-world viral challenges, age-inappropriate content, or unauthorized copying of protected names, characters, music, levels, or artwork. **Borrow the broad fantasy/mechanic; create original execution.**

## 3. Exact research process for each idea

1. **Discover 20–30 titles** from one source, sorted by plays/rating or a known successful trend. Note top 5 visually surprising mechanics.
2. **Prove outside-Roblox demand** with link, date, original platform, lifetime plays/downloads, rating **and** ratings count. Mark `PASS`, `WATCHLIST`, or `FAIL` against Section 1A.
3. **Find ongoing content proof**: Are different creators playing it? Any big YouTuber? Shorts/TikToks with large views? What exact moment do viewers rewatch? Preserve video URLs and publication dates.
4. **Play the original for 10–15 minutes**, or inspect full gameplay if unavailable. Write the core loop, main fantasy, first 10 seconds, what makes it funny, and what makes players repeat.
5. **Search Roblox in 5 ways**: exact title, verb/action, fantasy, alternate synonyms, and genre. Look at direct and adjacent versions. Document evidence rather than declaring it nonexistent.
6. **Design the simplification**: remove systems, turn precise gestures into one tap/hold/drag, add readable rewards, exaggerated cartoon feedback, and one friend interaction.
7. **Create the hook**: one-line Roblox title, one clear thumbnail scene, a 3-second TikTok moment. If the screenshot doesn't communicate the game, reconsider.
8. **Sketch the MVP**: smallest playable map, one mechanic, server-side scoring, one upgrade, one multiplayer encounter, minimal mobile UI. Estimate build complexity.
9. **Score using the rubric below**, with the original and Roblox links. Build only when evidence and implementation line up.
10. **Playtest with 3–5 people** (ideally unfamiliar with the concept). Watch confusion; do not explain controls unless asked. Record 10-second comprehension, first laugh/clip, first replay, and whether they choose another round.
11. **After launch**, optimize for genuine retention and repeat play rather than only total visits or bought traffic.

### What Roblox actually rewards

Roblox's published discovery guidance emphasizes **play-through rate, early bounce rate, play days per user, playtime per user, and meaningful co-play**. It also advises **unique, accurate titles/thumbnails**, and warns that near-duplicate experiences can get reduced exposure. It does **not** establish a guaranteed viral formula. [Official Roblox Creator Hub](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery).

### Taller aplicado del 21/11/2026 — proceso reutilizable de idea a preproducción

**Regla de proceso adoptada para futuros conceptos:** seguir los pasos abajo en orden, sin confundir **validación de una palabra**, **demanda de un juego original**, **ausencia de competencia**, **diversión del loop** y **aprobación de inversión**. Son cinco verificaciones independientes. Una palabra conocida o popular no aprueba automáticamente el juego.

1. **Abrir el vault ANTES de proponer.** `README.md → AGENTS.md → GameIdeasForRoblox.md`, y `CampaingsAndPreGameCreation.md` §1–2 si el trabajo incluye nombres/thumbnail. Revisar backlog y proyectos previos para no repetir ideas descartadas.
2. **Extraer fantasías de éxitos externos, no inventar demanda.** Explorar Flash (Armor Games, Flashpoint, Kongregate), Coolmath, CrazyGames, Poki, Steam, Xbox, móviles, minijuegos sociales y videos. Registrar juego original, URL, cantidad y tipo de partidas/descargas, rating, cantidad de votos, fecha y creadores que siguen jugando. Preferir >3 M y rating ≥4.8/5, o ≥9/10 con ≥200k votos. Excepciones = WATCHLIST hasta justificarlas.
3. **Construir 5–10 candidatos distintos.** Cada uno debe explicarse con una frase del tipo «Soy X, hago Y para conseguir/evitar Z», un **verbo** jugable, acción inicial y recompensa instantánea. Favorecer reacción física, humor, amigos, momentos clippeables y MVP de 2–3 días. Mantener las ideas no elegidas con su estado para futura reutilización.
4. **Roblox market gap.** Consultar búsquedas por título exacto, verbo, fantasía, sinónimo y género. Para los competidores relevantes anotar URL, visitas, CCU, aprobación, thumbnail, tipo de juego, fecha y qué nos diferenciaría. **No** concluir que no existe competencia por no verla en una búsqueda; si hay un clon dominante sin diferencia real → NO-GO.
5. **Nombre por palabras (Creator Exchange + Roblox).** Comprobar **primero el verbo/palabra principal** en [Creator Exchange](https://creatorexchange.io/) con varios juegos independientes que demuestren visitas; capturar al menos tres ejemplos con URL/fecha/visitas. Si no hay demanda consistente, probar *Defend / Protect / Save* u otros sinónimos antes de comprometer nombre. Verificar nivel lector de cada palabra relevante con fuente explícita: **objetivo 3.º–4.º grado**. Luego revisar el título completo y similares en Roblox. No confundir facilidad de entender con éxito de la palabra.
6. **Diseñar el empaque ANTES de programar.** Elegir un título entendible y 2–3 conceptos de thumbnail. Composición base: **un protagonista + una acción física + una consecuencia visible**. Hacer prueba de lectura en tamaño móvil (3 segundos) y comprobar que la imagen promete gameplay real. Sin fantasy clara, título sólido ni miniatura legible → RESEARCH/NO-GO.
7. **Definir loop gris y prueba social.** Prototipo sin cosméticos ni mapas grandes: acción → feedback instantáneo → recompensa → mejora → repetir. Primera acción ~5–10 s, primer premio objetivo <30 s, primer momento sorpresa <60 s. Debe tener diversión aunque se eliminen las recompensas cosméticas y ser jugable solo. Probar con 3–5 usuarios sin explicar las reglas. Si falla, corregir gameplay **antes** de pulir arte, monetización o anuncios.
8. **Analytics-first y semáforo de decisión.** Cada feature nueva requiere hipótesis, KPI, denominador, evento comprobable por servidor, QA, versión y comparación. Para el MVP medir entrada→primer golpe/acción→primer premio→primera ronda→replay; D1 y sesión mediante dashboard nativo después de publicar. Decidir **GO (sólo con gates completos) / RESEARCH (faltan pruebas) / NO-GO (falla crucial)**. Aprobación del concepto no es aprobación para publicar ni gastar en Ads.

**Registro mínimo por propuesta:** fecha; inspiración y URL; evidencia externa; competidores Roblox; keyword principal y tres ejemplos Creator Exchange; nivel lector y fuente; core fantasy; título; 2–3 thumbnails; loop 30 s; jugabilidad social/móvil; 3 momentos grabables; MVP 2–3 días; analytics; decisión y próximo test. Si el propietario hizo una validación pero no compartió cifras, poner `VALIDACIÓN REPORTADA POR EL USUARIO; DATOS NO ARCHIVADOS`, nunca un número inventado.

**Aprendizaje de la sesión:** analizamos opciones de lucha/reflejos, expansión de multitudes, ejércitos de juguetes, lanzamiento/física, defensa de objetos, carreras de patos, exploración, destrucción y trampas sociales. Se eligió **Defend the Pizza!** porque la promesa «proteger una pizza gigante de monstruos» es fácil de transmitir, no porque haya evidencia de retención real. La persona usuaria informó que comprobó la keyword `Defend` en Creator Exchange y su legibilidad Grade 3–4; se aceptó el nombre sin repetirle la misma pregunta. Los enlaces, métricas y capturas de ese análisis no están archivados todavía. Las tres thumbnails son ideas, no tests A/B, y el prototipo aún no se jugó.

## 4. Game concept scorecard (100 points)

First apply the **hard filters**. Never use score alone to wave through an unverified idea.

| Factor | Max | What earns the points |
|---|---:|---|
| Demonstrated demand outside Roblox | 20 | Proven high plays/downloads, high rating, large rating count, and current creator/audience signals |
| Roblox market gap / clear differentiation | 20 | No dominant exact equivalent, or a truly underserved angle after systematic search |
| Core loop & fantasy | 20 | One-sentence fantasy, fun 30-second loop, immediate feedback, repeatable upgrade/progression |
| Funny / social / shareable | 15 | Friends make it better, surprising failures, instantly clippable moments |
| Understandable to kids | 10 | 3-second thumbnail, 10-second onboarding, one clear control, mobile-friendly |
| Fast, cheap MVP | 10 | Playable 2–3-day loop, few systems/assets, reusable components |
| Non-exploitative progression/monetization potential | 5 | Honest cosmetics, optional convenience, fair upgrades; reason to return |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | |

- **85–100:** High priority **only if all hard filters pass**.
- **70–84:** Prototype after resolving the top remaining risk.
- **55–69:** Watchlist; revisit if new evidence arrives.
- **Below 55:** Archive.
- **Unknown evidence:** Use `?`, not an invented zero or inflated score. Mark `INCOMPLETE`.

**Fast kill test:** Can we make a fun, recognizable 30-second clip without a tutorial? If not, drop or rethink the adaptation.

### First things worth investigating deeper

1. **Swords and Souls -> silly skill-training party game:** Exceptionally strong original rating, recognizable upgrades, very short minigames. **Risk:** Roblox already offers training simulators. Our version must rely on genuinely fun skill challenges and social duels rather than AFK clicking.
2. **Infectonator 2 -> harmless chain-reaction crowd simulator:** Big readable chain reactions and highly shareable outcomes. **Risk:** must verify Roblox competition, avoid age-inappropriate horror, and make it distinct.
3. **Jacksmith -> goofy forge-with-friends:** A strong social crafting fantasy, but **fails** the current rating threshold and already has Roblox neighbors; could be reconsidered only with clear additional demand and a distinct twist.

## 6. Roblox adaptation formulas

Start with the emotional promise, not the old game's art:

- **Physics launch:** `launch -> crash/fly farther -> currency -> bigger gadget -> friends compete`. Amplifier: ragdoll, absurd objects, weather surprises.
- **Chain reactions:** `trigger one thing -> NPC/object cascade -> rewards -> bigger chain -> friends sabotage`. Amplifier: scale and ridiculous visual transformations.
- **Obby/race:** `queue -> 60-second round -> hilarious obstacle/failure -> earn cosmetics or speed effects -> rematch`. Amplifier: friends can activate the traps.
- **Tycoon/simulator:** `do one recognizable job -> visible mess/profit -> purchase faster tool -> automate a little -> build/show off`. Amplifier: chaotic visitors and rivals.
- **Bluffing party game:** `secret role/choice -> public bet -> reveal -> group laughs -> rematch`. Amplifier: short rounds, clear tells and reveals; no need for realistic deception tech.
- **Challenge as a fictional object:** `build tension -> press/hold -> cartoon watermelon pops / object explodes safely -> scores -> new challenge`. Amplifier: anticipation and reactions, **not real-world dangerous instructions**.
- **2D translated to Roblox:** use side-view camera, simple plane-constrained movement, and mobile-friendly big buttons when the original mechanic is inherently 2D. 3D isn't mandatory.

**Dumb it down, don't drain it:** Keep the one unforgettable action; simplify controls and explanation, not the satisfying payoff.

### IDEA-005 — Defend the Pizza! (decisión del propietario 2026-11-21)

**Documento de diseño activo:** [Projects/DefendThePizza/PREPRODUCTION_V01.md](Projects/DefendThePizza/PREPRODUCTION_V01.md) — V0.1, propuesta de combate físico, robo/recuperación, storyboard de thumbnail, first-minute, prototipo por gates y analítica; no es código ni asset final.

- **Nombre elegido:** `Defend the Pizza!` (no es un lanzamiento ni una reserva exclusiva del título).
- **Validación reportada por el usuario:** se investigó `Defend` en Creator Exchange y el nivel de comprensión del término es aproximadamente Grade 3–4. **No** se adjuntaron números, capturas, URL de los juegos analizados ni fuente de grade-level a esta actualización; por lo tanto se reconoce la decisión del usuario, pero la evidencia cuantitativa queda `UNKNOWN` hasta documentarse. No inventar un PASS de 3M/4.8/competencia.
- **Core fantasy propuesta:** proteger una pizza gigante de monstruos hambrientos con amigos.
- **Diferenciación propuesta:** defensa física activa con espátulas/empujones, enemigos cómicos y mordidas que alteran visiblemente la pizza; no tower defense pasivo.
- **Core loop propuesto:** repeler monstruos → salvar porciones → conseguir monedas → mejorar herramienta/defensa → siguiente oleada; rondas cortas y posibilidad de jugar solo.
- **Thumbnail por validar:** pizza enorme en centro, monstruo mordiéndola, jugador desplazando al atacante con espátula; 1 protagonista/1 acción/1 consecuencia legibles en miniatura.
- **MVP sugerido, no autorizado para implementación todavía:** una cocina, una pizza con daño visible, 3 spawns, espátula, 3 arquetipos simples, 5 oleadas, 1 upgrade, servidor autoritativo + first-hit/first-wave/replay analytics y móvil desde la primera iteración.
- **Pendientes del gate:** confirmar competidores específicos en Roblox, preservar evidencia de Creator Exchange, seleccionar thumbnail, jugar prototipo gris divertido antes de construir a escala o invertir en Ads.
- **No iniciar:** gasto en ads, monetización ni publicación sin aprobación explícita.
